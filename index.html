Tourist Spots in Bontoc

Here are the top 5 destinations to explore around Bontoc.

🌄 TOPS (Bontoc Overlook Point)

"TOPS offers an incredible panoramic view of Bontoc and the surrounding landscapes, making it a favorite spot for sunrise and sunset views."

Visitors describe TOPS as the ultimate scenic overlook in town.

Search on Google Maps

💦 Alejos Falls

Relax by the cascading waters of Alejos Falls. Read more in The Natural Wonders of Bontoc travel guide.

Search on Google Maps

🌊 Bontoc Dike

A peaceful riverfront spot perfect for afternoon walks, jogging, and hanging out with friends by the water.

Search on Google Maps

☕ Ness Presso

Visit the local coffee hub, NPC, for fresh brews and a cozy atmosphere.

Search on Google Maps

🌾 Farmfinity Resort

An agritourism resort in Santa Cruz, Bontoc showcasing sustainable agricultural practices, organic produce, and family-friendly activities.

Search on Google Maps
